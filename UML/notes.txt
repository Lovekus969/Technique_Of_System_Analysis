What is Multiplicity (UML)?
  Multiplicity specifies how many instances of one class can be associated with a single instance of another class.
What does .. mean in UML?

.. means “range” or “from–to”

It is used mainly in multiplicity to specify a lower and upper bound.
What does * mean in UML?

* means “many” or “unbounded”

In UML, * is shorthand for:
